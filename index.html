Alerts on iPhone

iPhones only deliver alerts to sites added to the home screen. One-time setup:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open JoshChat from the new icon on your home screen.
  4. Sign in there with the same account. Your chats come with you; tap Turn on when it asks about notifications.

The home-screen app keeps its own sign-in, which is why you sign in again inside it. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer.
