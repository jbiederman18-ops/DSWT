Alerts on iPhone

iPhones only deliver alerts to sites added to the home screen. One-time setup:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open DS(WT) Crew from the new icon on your home screen.
  4. Join the crew there with code and tap the 🔔 bell at the top.

The home-screen app keeps its own sign-in, which is why you join the crew again inside it. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer.
